# Primary Baseline Rewrite & v0.1–v0.4 Archival — 2026-11-11

## 1. Positioning as Primary Baseline

On 11 November 2026, following an end-to-end reviewer audit of the experimental evidence and manuscript style, the Paper Track manuscript was completely rewritten and promoted to the **primary paper baseline** ([`../manuscript.md`](../manuscript.md)):

- **Title**: *Knowing the Bug Is Not Landing the Fix: Why Execution Feedback Stalls Before Patch Delivery in Budget-Constrained Coding Agents*
- **Narrative shift**: Eliminated repetitive defensive disclaimers and formulaic phrasing; unified the whole empirical program (**3,517 runs and shared-draft realizations across 95 tasks from 15 repositories; 3,949 graded endpoints**: Study A with 736 end-to-end runs, Study B with 216 shared-draft continuations / 864 model sessions / 648 graded endpoints, and three 855-run follow-up prompt-mitigation ablation cycles totaling 2,565 runs) around a single explanatory mechanism—the **Four-Stage Attrition Pipeline** between diagnostic feedback and delivered repository fixes.
- **Archival of v0.1–v0.4**: Previous drafts and legacy artifacts remain in the main project archive; this companion contains the current paper release.

## 2. Core Empirical Additions in the Baseline Rewrite & Deep-Dive Refinement (`v0.4 review 5`)

1. **Stage 1 — Command-Launch Attrition (`python3 -B PY`)**:
   - In the final configuration (`R03`/`R04`, 108 check-construction attempts), 85 produce execution output, but **38 of 108 (35%)** fail before importing the target repository because the constructor omits `- <<` in `python3 -B - << 'PY'`, causing Python to search for a nonexistent file named `PY`. Only 20 of 108 reach target code with publicly grounded expectations (13 consistent, 7 counterevidence).
2. **Stage 2 — Action Substitution, Stage 1→2 Bridge, and Silent `read_file` Resets**:
   - Directly aggregated all executed ordinary tool calls across all 108 `R03`/`R04` continuation packets ([`../supplement/tool-allocation-v04.csv`](../supplement/tool-allocation-v04.csv), exported via [`../scripts/build_v04_review_analysis.py`](../scripts/build_v04_review_analysis.py)).
   - On the 64 failing initial drafts `D`, pre-supplying execution output (`F`) cuts common-check replay in half relative to `C` (`46/64` vs `22/64`, $72\% \to 34\%$), saving 29 `run_command` calls ($226 \to 197$) and 11 `get_status` calls ($17 \to 6$), but converts them into **30 additional `read_file` calls ($239 \to 269$)**.
   - Across `R03`/`R04`, **58 executed `read_file` calls (`25` C, `33` F; `24` on failing drafts)** pass a malformed optional pagination key with a stray quote (`"start_line\"": "151"`). Because `start_line` is optional, the tool ignores it and **silently resets to lines 1–150 in 56 of 58 calls (`24` C, `32` F)**.
   - Stratifying by check quality (Table 2) links Stage 1 to Stage 2: in `Other output` ($N=65$, including the 38 `python3 -B PY` crashes), `60% (39/65)` of `F` branches must re-run or repair the check; in `Grounded target` ($N=20$), `F` replay drops **80% (`20/20` to `4/20`)**, call-0 `read_file` starts jump from **`6/20` (30%) to `16/20` (80%)**, and edit-attempting branches double from **`4/20` to `8/20`**—yet 4 of 8 hit `BudgetExceeded`.
   - Sharing one 8-call pool means **83% of `C` branches (`53/64`) and 89% of `F` branches (`57/64`) on failing drafts exhaust the 8-tool cap (`BudgetExceeded`)**, leaving retained edits (`8/64` vs `8/64`) and confirmed passes (`3/63` vs `3/64`) identical.
3. **Stage 3 — Complete Four-Outcome Anatomy of the Counterevidence Cohort**:
   - Across the seven `R03`/`R04` counterevidence realizations (eight across all batches, adding `FastAPI13537/R02`), **6 of 7 `F` branches (`7/8` overall)** issue a concrete `edit_file` call across four deterministic outcomes:
     1. *Landed within cap (`2/7` F in `R03`/`R04`, `3/8` overall; 100% pass)*: `Requests6592/R03` (C & F), `Rich3067/R03` (F), `FastAPI13537/R02` (F).
     2. *Exact, uniquely matching fixes preempted by one slot (`2/7` F: `Rich3518/R04` and `FastAPI13786/R04`, plus `Rich3518/R04` C)*: Static verification against `D` proves both have an exact unique match (`occurrences = 1`) fixing the failing assertion (`highlight=self.highlight` at line 456 of `rich/table.py` after F lost 1 slot and C lost 2 slots to silent `start_line"` resets; `self.model.in_.value` on `fastapi/security/api_key.py` at event 9 right after an event-8 `grep` hit `BudgetExceeded`).
     3. *Multi-part tasks cut off mid-localization (`2/7` F: `FastAPI14986/R03` and `FastAPI15800/R04`, plus `FastAPI15800/R04` C)*: Two-file tasks interrupted at event 8 emit truncated event-9 edits on `applications.py` (bare `"] = True,"` leaving `docs.py` unedited, or overwriting `def include_router(` / `def __init__(` leaving `routing.py` unedited).
     4. *Cut off at target-file entry (`1/7` F: `FastAPI15589/R04`)*: Spends 6 calls on `params.py` (including a silent `"start_line\"": "600"` reset), locates `convert_underscores` in `fastapi/dependencies/utils.py` via `grep` at events 6–7, and has its first `read_file` on `utils.py` (lines 750–850) blocked by `BudgetExceeded` at event 8 (`46.7` s elapsed, `103.3` s remaining).
4. **Stage 4 — Delimiter Corruption (`93/93` Swallowed `old_string:`) & Continuation Regression**:
   - Across `R03`/`R04`, `C` and `F` incur **45 and 48 (93 total, all on failing drafts) `edit_file` rejections** for missing `old_string` across **11 branches** (`6` C, `5` F; mean `8.5`/branch, peaking at 17 in `Rich3067/R03` C and 24 in `Rich3718/R04` F).
   - Inspecting raw arguments proves **93 of 93 rejected calls (100%) actually emitted `old_string:`** fused into `new_string` when editing code containing quotes, colons, or regexes, and **82 of 93 (88%)** split target code on `:` and `,` into bogus keys. Because schema errors do not decrement the 8-call cap and report only `old_string` as missing, the model repeats the corrupted call in an autoregressive loop.
   - Across all 432 continuation branches, 29 retain executable diffs over `D`, and 4 of 29 delete working draft logic (`Requests7433/R03`).
5. **Table 3 & Architectural Synthesis with Follow-Up 855-Run Cycles (`2,565` Runs)**:
   - Added **Table 3** ([`../tables/prompt-ablations-v04.tex`](../tables/prompt-ablations-v04.tex)) summarizing three 855-run prompt-only mitigation cycles across all 95 tasks and 15 repositories (`2,565` runs):
     - *Subagent soft caps* (`2026-10-25`, 855 runs): `89–96%` overrun 4–8 tool caps (median `21–24` requests; `25` vs `19/22` in R03).
     - *Schema/heredoc retry* (`2026-10-15`, 855 runs): `3/33` full recoveries in R03 (`0` pass; `0.0 pp` official, `−2.4/−5.1 pp` external).
     - *Voluntary `keep`/`reject`* (`2026-10-23`, 855 runs): `23` `keep` and **`0` `reject` calls** across 190 runs (`+2.0 pp` official, `−2.4 pp` external).
